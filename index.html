Four things it does well

Not forty features — the handful that carry the workload

The tool ships with a long feature list. In practice, these are the four that justify keeping it in the workflow.

Background removal
Use case 01

Cut out a background in one pass

Point it at a portrait or product shot and it isolates the subject — hair edges included, which is where cheap tools usually fall apart. Export transparent for overlays, or drop in a solid color for a marketplace listing.

Portrait retouch
Use case 02

Soften skin without the plastic look

The retouch pass evens out blemishes and smooths skin while keeping texture, so faces don't turn into wax. Good enough for a headshot or a profile photo you didn't want to book a studio for.

Object cleanup
Use case 03

Erase the thing that ruined the shot

A stranger walking through the frame, a trash can, a stray cable. Brush over it and the fill reconstructs what should have been behind it. The travel-photo-saver most people didn't know they needed.

Auto color & exposure
Use case 04

Fix flat, dull, or badly-lit photos

One click balances color and rescues an over- or under-exposed frame. Not a substitute for shooting it right, but it turns "unusable" into "fine for the site" faster than fiddling with sliders.
